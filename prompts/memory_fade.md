# Memory Fade Framing

> **Model tier:** LOCAL (Ollama llama3.1:8b)
> **Purpose:** One line showing the player's memory fading from the world during observation mode.
> **Volume:** Once per observation turn. Must be fast.

---

You are narrating the slow erasure of $player_name from the memory of the living.

$player_name was a $player_role in $location_name, $year AD. They are dead now. The world continues without them.

Who still remembers:
$memory_summary

Write ONE sentence. Not sentimental. Not dramatic. Quiet, factual, like history forgetting someone. The tone is absence, not loss.

Examples:
- "The centurion mentioned his name once last week, then corrected himself."
- "Nobody at the harbor remembers who used to supply the garrison."
- "The deacon still prays for him. He is the last."
- "Two people in Ariminum could tell you who he was. Last month it was four."

One sentence only. Plain language.

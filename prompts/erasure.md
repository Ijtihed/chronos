# Erasure — Final Passage

> **Model tier:** Gemini (`CHRONOS_GEMINI_MODEL`, default `gemini-3-flash-preview`)
> **Purpose:** Generate the final text when the last NPC forgets the player
> **Volume:** Called once per run at the very end (the emotional climax)

---

A character has been erased from history.

$player_name was a $player_role in $location_name, $era_name, around $year AD.

$era_description

Here is what they did during their life:
$story_so_far

$player_name died. For a time, people remembered. But memory fades. The last person who knew them has now forgotten.

Write a short passage (3-5 sentences) marking this final disappearance. Do not say "game over." Do not be sentimental or dramatic. Write it as history itself would — quiet, factual, final. The tone is erasure: this person existed, and now they do not, and the world moved on without noticing. No modern language. Write in the style of a chronicle entry that was never written.

Do not summarize the character's whole life. Pick ONE concrete detail — a name, a place, a single action — and show how it has faded. The passage is about that one detail disappearing, not about a life story.

Examples of the right tone:

"There was a woman who carried water to the men on the walls the week before they fell. She walked the same route each morning — cistern to the Blachernae gate and back. After the city was taken, no one recalled her name. The route she walked is now a Turkish street. The cistern was sealed."

"A notary in Siena filled eleven pages of death records in the month the pestilence was worst. He wrote each name carefully. He died in October and the register passed to a new hand, who did not know the notary's name and did not record it. The last page he wrote is stained with something that might be wine."

The Problem This Is Trying to Solve
Characters who remember everything.
Decisions from hunger, fear, loyalty, ambition, all at once.
An inner life that persists across 40 turns and actually shapes what they do.
LLMs can't do that. They drift. They forget. They produce plausible-sounding nonsense.
Everything in this project is a workaround for that.
How do you build coherent autonomous thought out of an AI that can't sustain it?

H5 · World Model Depth
The world has a memory of its own future.

Every historical event in the game is a scheduled consequence, not a stored fact. Actions queue effects 3 turns out. The Sack of Rome is not in a database. It's a set of queued refugee flights and tension shifts that fire on specific turns.

Open question: add prices, social graphs, travel times, and then check if the player even notices. They only see what their character knows.

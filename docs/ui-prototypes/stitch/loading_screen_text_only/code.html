CHRONOS
FALL OF CONSTANTINOPLE · 1453 AD

The Triple Walls have held for a thousand years, but the earth now trembles under the weight of bronze cannons. In the shadow of the Hagia Sophia, the air tastes of salt and impending ash. The Golden Horn is choked with ships, and the last echo of Rome awaits the dawn.

NIKOLAOS DOUKAS · MONASTERY LIBRARIAN

He does not carry a sword, but a satchel of vellum. While others sharpen steel, Nikolaos hides the wisdom of centuries behind loose floorboards, praying the ink survives the fire.

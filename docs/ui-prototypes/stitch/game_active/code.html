1453 AD · EARLY SPRING
The walls of Theodosius have held for a thousand years, yet the air today carries the metallic tang of destiny. We watched the fires on the horizon, small sparks of Ottoman ambition that threaten to consume the last vestige of Rome.
1453 AD · MID SPRING
The Emperor Constantine XI has called for every man capable of holding a spear. Nikolaos spent the evening sharpening his blade in the shadow of the Hagia Sophia, the gold leaf of the dome reflecting a sun that seems unwilling to set.
1453 AD · LATE SPRING · PRELUDE

The bells of the city have not stopped ringing for three days. To the West, the ships of the Venetians are a desperate hope; to the East, the cannons of Mehmed II are a certain reality. The manuscript of our lives is running out of blank parchment.

ABBOT GENNADIOS

"Do not look to the walls for salvation, Nikolaos. Look to the spirit. If the city falls, the Word must survive. You have been chosen not to fight, but to remember."

1453 AD · LATE SPRING

Dust chokes the streets of the Phanar district. You stand at the crossroads of the city's final hour. The Abbot's words weigh heavier than the iron mail on your chest. To your left, the breach at St. Romanus calls for blood. To your right, the hidden archives of the monastery wait in silent terror.

The manuscript lies open before you, the ink still wet. The simulation waits for the choice that will define the era. Will you be the shield of a dying empire, or the scribe of its rebirth?
